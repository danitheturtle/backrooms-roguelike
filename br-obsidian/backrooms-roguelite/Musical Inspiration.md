Every track is haunted. 90s mall, old cartoons, nostalgia, elevator music, haunted house, grandma's radio station.

There will be rooms that spawn with a device that makes music. A radio, a gramaphone, an old PC, a CRT television, etc. I want them to play slightly distorted music that still somehow sounds familiar, like it could be on any elevator ride you've ever taken before its sound got fucked up and played through a shitty speaker. I want these tracks to be something that draws you in to an important part of a level and keeps you feeling uneasy while you're there.

I wanna make effective use of tense silence. Maybe many minutes of quiet only broken by a short 5-10 note sting of creepy emotion then back to silence.

"10 hours of silence broken up by windows start noises" vibes.

And it would be fun to play with the "silence". Not all silence is equal. maybe some white noise of flourecent lights. faint footsteps you can't quite hear. A low dull buzzing that makes the brain uncomfortable.
