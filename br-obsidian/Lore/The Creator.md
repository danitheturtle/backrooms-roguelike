An extra-dimensional, plural being with no physical form that feeds on curiosity and creativity.

It/Its, They/Them/Theirs

Its created this dimension between worlds as something akin to a venus fly trap. An irresistable labyrinth of nostalgia bait. The more you explore and try to understand its trap, the more complex it becomes (its sometimes called The Complex for this reason).

It only lets you find your way out when its done feeding and knows you will return so it can feed again.

Staying still and doing nothing, or trying the same thing over and over again, causes it to get upset.

This monster works on many, many levels. Its in control of this dimension, so it can explain random shifts or changes in the level. Its using nostalgia as bait, so it can use recursive backrooms nostalgia in its favor, recreating iconic parts of backrooms lore without that lore being fully in-universe.

Its able to read thoughts through a fungus-like growth whos spores that once inhaled, let it read your thoughts and drain you more efficiently
