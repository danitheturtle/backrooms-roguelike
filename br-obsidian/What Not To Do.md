There are a lot of backrooms games. Most of them are trash.

- note pickups that don't leave the player with questions. Don't just throw lore at the player. A note pickup should be a reward for the investigation gameplay loop
- random noises that don't exist in 3d space aren't interesting. A noise needs a source that can be pinpointed
- Time pressure
- heavy film grain, chromatic abberation, VHS filter, or other post-effects
- One big flat doom-style level
